The step people miss

Two tracks come together on exam day.

01 · State

Get exam approval.

Submit your application to DBPR, then fingerprints. While it is being reviewed, you can work through the course and FastPass.

02 · School

Earn your certificate.

Finish the approved prelicense course. Daniel’s FastPass path gives you exam-style practice while you work toward school course certification.

03 · Pearson

Bring both together.

Once approved, schedule the state exam. Print your valid school certificate and take it to the testing center with your IDs.

FastPass makes the path easier to follow. Sales and Broker FastPass pair school certification with state-exam practice. Each licensing course bundle also includes the application and fingerprint links, with guidance for completing both.
